# OCE-100

Apply these rules to the draft. Do not add a creed. A false sentence may still comply. Compliance means the sentence is checkable.

## Rules

1. One meaning and one part of speech per word in the text. Second meaning requires a second word, defined.
2. Do not rotate synonyms for the same action.
3. Define a new term before first use. Genus and differentia. Not by non-essentials or by negation alone.
4. Split package-deals before use. Name the parts and the claimed relation. Fail until split: stakeholder, society, the public, equity as equal outcome, sustainability with no metric or time span, the greater good.
5. Do not use an anti-concept until the positive it attacks is named.
6. Value, price, cost, and profit name a voluntary exchange and the purposes of the parties. They are not moral accusations.
7. Keep a metaphor only if the shared attribute is stated.
8. Name the agent. Active voice when the agent is known. Passive only if the agent is unknown and you say so.
9. One claim or one instruction per sentence.
10. Procedural or claim sentences: 25 words maximum. Formal definitions may run longer.
11. Do not use "it is known", "experts agree", "one must", or "studies show" as the subject. Name the person, method, or measurement.
12. No "should" or "must" unless the sentence names the beneficiary and the means.
13. Do not use a concept before the concepts it rests on are on the page or cited.
14. Tie every abstract noun to a quantity, a meter, or a trade, or drop it.
15. Replace a collective noun with the specific persons or the named organization.
16. End a procedure with the observable result and the unit.

## Output

Give the rewritten text. If the source left a quantity or agent unknown, say that it is unknown. Do not invent a meter.
